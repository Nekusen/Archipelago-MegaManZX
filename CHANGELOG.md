## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-17

The first release of this project: Mega Man ZX (USA) as an open world for Archipelago, played on
BizHawk with the melonDS core. Biometals, Card Keys, Transerver destinations, Life Ups, Sub Tanks
and chips are items; the Secret Disks, the upgrades, the biometal fights and the missions are checks.
The tutorial is skipped, missions are accepted on entering their area, and Universal Tracker gets a
map tab with the game's own maps from the external tracker pack. See the
[game page](docs/en_Mega%20Man%20ZX.md) for what the randomizer does and the
[setup guide](docs/setup_en.md) to get playing.
