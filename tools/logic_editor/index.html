Shortcuts and usage

Keyboard

VSelect tool
PDraw polygon (click adds a vertex; click on the first one, double click or Enter closes; Esc cancels; right click removes the last one)
ACreate connections on the canvas: drag from one region to another (or click the source and click the target). Bidirectional; with Shift, one way only. Main is ignored: outside the polygons nothing happens
EscCancel / deselect / close panels
DelDelete the selection (connection, region, hand-placed position) or the active vertex
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo
0 · + · -Fit · zoom in · zoom out (also the mouse wheel)
FSearch (jumps to the room and centers the node)
?This panel
Space+dragPan the canvas (also middle button or dragging over an empty area)

Mouse

  • Click selects (node, connection, region). Hand-placed nodes (hexagon) can be dragged.
  • With a region selected: drag its vertices; double click on a side inserts a vertex; right click or Del on a vertex removes it (minimum 3); Shift+drag inside moves the whole region.
  • Left panel: drag an item from "Unplaced" onto the canvas (or press "Place" and click on the canvas).

Semantics

  • A node belongs to the smallest polygon that contains it; if none, to Main. It can be pinned by hand (pushpin).
  • Requirement = alternatives (OR) of atoms (AND). expert EXTENDS normal: in expert the alternatives of both tiers count.
  • Expressions: HX & (LX | FX), and/or, +, ,, free/any, never, LIFEUP>=2.
  • Everything saves itself (600 ms after the last change) and the server returns the validation report.
